Pengu Jump

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Pengu Jump is a game made by Aulonasoft (Italy). This page explains what information the game uses, who receives it and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to aulonasoft@gmail.com.

In short

  • You don't need an account. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • We don't run servers that receive your data, and we never sell it.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked first.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Online rankings, if you choose to sign in, are run by Google Play Games or Apple Game Center.

1. Information saved on your device

The game saves your best height, coins, skins, missions, daily challenge results, your recent runs and your settings on your device, together with a short log of technical errors that helps the game recover from problems. This information is not sent to us. On Android, if you have turned on device backup, Android may include it in your backup to your Google account, under Google's terms. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it from the device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: now and then between runs, and when you choose to watch one (for example to continue a run or double your coins). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (on iPhone only if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived;
  • information about your device and the app (for example model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads are personalised; you can change it at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, Apple asks whether the app may track you; the game works the same either way. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings. The Remove ads purchase stops the ads between runs.

Google processes this information under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases

Purchases (Remove ads, Starter Pack, Golden Pengu) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The game only receives confirmation of what you bought, so it can unlock it and restore it later. We never receive your name, card or other payment details.

4. Online rankings (Google Play Games, Apple Game Center)

Online rankings are optional. If you sign in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone), the game sends your best height and your daily challenge score to that service. They are shown to other players with your player name and picture, according to your settings in that service, and the game shows you the rankings and your friends' scores. Google and Apple process this information under their own privacy policies. You can play everything without signing in, and you can delete your Play Games data in the Play Games settings.

5. Sharing

When you tap Share, the game draws a picture of your score and passes it, with a short message, to the app you choose. Nothing is sent to us.

6. Usage statistics and crash reports

The game has a Usage statistics setting. At present the game does not send usage statistics or crash reports anywhere. If that changes, the information will be anonymous (no names, contacts or location), it will only be sent while the setting is on, and this policy will be updated first.

7. Children

Pengu Jump is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Legal bases, transfers and how long information is kept

Personalised ads are shown only with your consent where the law requires it; other ads, measurement and fraud prevention rely on the legitimate interest in offering the game for free. Purchases are processed to fulfil your order. Google and Apple may process information outside the European Union using the safeguards described in their policies. Information on your device stays until you delete it; Google and Apple keep information according to their own policies.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent at any time. Because we don't hold your information on our own servers, most of these choices are made directly in the game, in your device settings, or with Google and Apple, but we will gladly help: write to aulonasoft@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority; in Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
